## What you may do, which is nearly everything

**Fork the system and put your own name and mark on it.** That is the intended
use, and it costs you nothing.

The tokens, the stylesheets, the scripts and the component code are Apache-2.0.
Take all of it. Change the colours, keep them, publish the result, sell the
result. The only thing you need to do is replace the identity with your own.

You may also, without asking:

- Refer to **{NAME}** by name when talking about it, reviewing it, comparing it,
  or crediting it. That is nominative use and needs no permission.
- Show the mark in a screenshot of the software or the documentation.
- Link to `{site}`.

## What needs a yes first

- Putting the mark, wordmark, tile or icons on anything you make.
- Naming your own project, product, package or service in a way that suggests it
  is **{NAME}**, or is endorsed by it.
- Registering a domain, an account, or a package name containing the name.
- Modifying the mark. It has no permitted modification: it is not recoloured, not
  shadowed, not stretched.

Ask: **{email}**. A plain description of what you want to do is enough.

---

## Why it is split this way

A design system is worth more the more people use it. An identity is worth
nothing at all once two people use it, because its whole job is to tell one
person's work from another's.

So the system is given away and the identity is kept. Both decisions serve the
same goal, from opposite directions.
