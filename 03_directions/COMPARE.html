Four directions for Aninda Studio

Each one is a different answer at the root — a different mark logic, a different colour philosophy, a different typographic voice. They are not four shades of one idea, and they are deliberately not equally safe.

Every colour on this page was computed and measured by 05_colour/engine.py and read from its proof files. No hex value and no contrast ratio was typed by hand anywhere. Each direction ships four themes — light, dark, high-contrast light, high-contrast dark — and every text pairing in every theme clears WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1), or AAA (7:1) in the high-contrast themes, measured on the rounded 8-bit hex and then re-measured with every channel of both colours nudged by ±1. The published figure is the worst of those.

Type is provisional: the pairings shown are plausible candidates so the directions can be compared with real letterforms in both scripts. The final typeface decision is being made separately, on rendered and measured specimens.

estuaryEstuary মোহনা

Quiet editorial competence. A deep green-teal ground against a bone paper, with the warmth coming from the paper rather than from a second expressive colour. Barishal sits in a river delta; an estuary is where fresh water meets salt, which is also what a studio serving two audiences in two scripts is doing. This is Direction A: the existing draft, corrected. One correction is substantive — the draft declares no accent at all, and a system whose accent equals its text colour cannot express a link, a selected row, or a primary action. A cyan-teal accent is therefore introduced: a near neighbour of the ground, discriminable from it, and still honouring 'no second expressive colour'.

sandhyaSandhya সন্ধ্যা

Rooted and literary. The mark is built from the Bangla letterform অ rather than the Latin 'a', so the identity is native to Bangla and the Latin wordmark is the companion — the reverse of how almost every bilingual brand is put together, and the arrangement your two audiences actually argue for. Colour is a warm ink, close to printing black on uncoated paper, with a single saturated vermilion signal. Vermilion because it is the colour of Bengali print culture and hand-set type, not because it is 'ethnic'; nothing here is folkloric. The register is a small press rather than a software company.

derivedDerived উদ্ভূত

The identity is an output of the system rather than a drawing placed beside it. The mark's proportions come from the type scale and from the measured contrast ratios, so regenerating the tokens regenerates the mark — which means the logo can never drift from the system it is supposed to represent. Colour is a wide analogous band through green and olive rather than a two-colour pair, so charts, categories and product sub-brands all have somewhere to live. This is the riskiest of the four: a generative identity is either genuinely distinctive or it ages very badly, and it is the hardest to keep disciplined once other people start using it.
