Identity01

The studio is Aninda Studio — one person, Aninda Sundar Howlader, in Barishal. The wordmark is lowercase, always: aninda studio. In running prose, sentence case is fine. The Bangla wordmark is অনিন্দ্য, the full spelling, cluster and all.

There is no tagline. There was one under the old name and it needed a rule to keep it under the wordmark at all times, which is a sign the name was not carrying its own weight. This name does. Products get their own names under the endorsement form: <Name> by Aninda Studio on first mention, then the name alone.

The mark02

A single-storey lowercase a: a circle tangent to a rising stem. The stem runs past the bowl, which is the only liberty taken with the letter and the thing that makes it a mark rather than a glyph.

Two weights, one geometry. Stroke 9 per 100 units at 24px and above; stroke 15 below. The circle centre, radius and stem position are identical in both, so it is the same mark getting heavier rather than a second drawing that can drift apart.

Clear space is half the mark’s own height on all four sides. Nothing enters it.

Icons03

Two artefacts, and the reason is mechanical rather than aesthetic. The web tile is a pre-rounded 22.46% squircle, because a browser will not round a favicon for you. The Apple master is a square, fully opaque 1024, because the system applies the radius itself and composites four appearance variants from it — default, dark, clear and tinted. Essential shapes stay inside 90 of the 100 units.

Do not bake a radius into the Apple master, and do not fake a bevel or a border. The platform adds the highlights.

Colour04

Estuary. A deep teal ground, a bone counterpoint, and nothing else expressive. Warmth comes from the paper, not from a second accent.

Seven tonal surface containers per theme carry depth. Four semantic hues, plus a secondary and a tertiary role so the token set maps onto a real product system.

Both themes are equal. [data-theme] is scoped to the attribute, never to :root, so a dark panel can sit inside a light page — and no attribute at all follows the reader’s system setting, which is what products actually ship.

Dynamic colour is not adopted. Material permits either; this system holds brand colour, because every pair here is computed against WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes and a wallpaper-derived scheme cannot be verified in advance.

Type05

Archivo for all Latin, using its width axis: 106 for display, 100 for text. Noto Sans Bengali for all Bangla, at 1.85 leading for the মাত্রা — never uppercased, never letter-spaced, never synthetically emboldened. JetBrains Mono for code, figures, labels and metadata. All three are SIL OFL 1.1.

The scale is a perfect fourth, 1.333, anchored at 16px and stated in rem. The jumps are large on purpose: hierarchy is unmistakable and you need fewer levels to express it. 16px is the floor for running prose in both scripts.

Space and shape06

Ten steps on a 4px base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128. When unsure, 16. Two things that belong together get a smaller gap than the gap to whatever comes next — that one rule does most of the work.

1216px container, 736px for prose, 24px gutters, 12 / 6 / 4 columns. Four radii: 4 for badges, 8 for controls, 14 for cards, 24 for heroes. The tile’s 22.46% is reused for nothing.

Components07

Filled controls, bordered containers, and one shadow used only for things that genuinely float — a menu, a toast, a dialog. In the dark theme that shadow resolves to none and a lighter surface carries the lift; a shadow on a dark ground reads as dirt.

Controls are 44px tall. Nothing tappable is under 24×24px, which is WCAG 2.2’s floor. Focus is a 3px ring at 2px offset plus a 1px inverse shadow, so it survives on pale surfaces and on the teal itself. Disabled is a token trio, never opacity — the label is the thing the reader is waiting on.

Nothing relies on colour alone. Every state carries a glyph and a word.

Motion08

120ms for colour, hover and focus. 220ms for something arriving or leaving. Nothing over 300ms. Ease-out for arrivals, ease-in-out for movement in place.

Material 3’s motion physics is not adopted, and that is a choice rather than an omission: springs are excellent on a platform that owns its frame budget, and this system targets low-end Android where a 220ms ease-out costs nothing. prefers-reduced-motion is honoured at the root.

The mark does not move. One exception: a 220ms fade on first page load, once.

Voice09

First person, because it is one person. Contractions where they fall naturally. Plain words. Say the number and its unit when there is one.

Say what a thing does, and name a constraint where it would otherwise surprise someone — not as a confession, just as information. Buttons are verbs with their object: Save the entry, not Submit. Errors say what happened and what happens next.

Bangla is written as Bangla, not translated from the English. Same steps, different sentences. English leads on developer surfaces; product surfaces carry both at equal weight.

Not yet — six things this system does not do

  • Role names are --as-*, not Material’s surface / onSurface vocabulary
  • No shape token scale — four fixed radii instead
  • No high-contrast theme
  • No forced-colors mode support
  • No screen-reader testing with a person who uses one
  • No user research of any kind
