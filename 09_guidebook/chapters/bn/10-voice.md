## The register

চলিত throughout. No সাধু forms anywhere in the product. That is the Academy's own
register: rule 2.11 replaces সাধু forms with চলিত ones outright, and the whole of
section 5 gives its ten verb paradigms exclusively in চলিত.

## Two rulings worth keeping in the head

**এখনো, not এখনও.** The Academy dictionary makes এখনও a bare cross-reference —
"দ্র এখনো" — to the full entry at এখনো.

**ছোটো, not ছোট, and বড়ো, not বড়.** Rule 2.3 names ছোটো in its own example list
alongside কালো, খাটো and ভালো, and both ছোটো and বড়ো are dictionary headwords
while ছোট and বড় have no entry at all.
