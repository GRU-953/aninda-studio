## The verified name strings

Both are proper names. Rule 4 of the Bangla Academy's 2012 spelling rules
exempts the names of people, institutions and organisations from the spelling
rules outright. Independently of that exemption, both are correctly formed:
স্টুডিও takes an initial conjunct under rule 2.9, in the same pattern as স্টেশন,
and a short ই-কার under rule 2.1.
